Ely.by — show your skin to others

Create a free account at ely.by with the same name as your in-game name, and enter its details here. Then press "Upload to Ely.by automatically" in the Skins tab to upload and activate the skin from the launcher directly. Note: for other players to see your skin, your Ely.by username must match your in-game name (change it at account.ely.by/profile/username).
